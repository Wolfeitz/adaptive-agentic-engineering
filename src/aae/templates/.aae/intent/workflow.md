Use evidence-driven, context-aware, plan-before-execution practices appropriate to the risk and complexity of the work.

## Progress telemetry

For bounded long-running work, keep substantive progress updates compact and expose real plan or runtime state: current phase, completed/total comparable units when meaningful, current activity, remaining work, next milestone, and blockers. Prefer measurable counts over estimated percentages. Do not infer completion from elapsed time or checklist counts when units vary materially in cost.
