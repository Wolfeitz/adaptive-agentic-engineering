# Bounded Context Builder

## Outcome

Create a fresh, minimal evidence packet that supports one named decision or task without importing irrelevant history or untrusted conclusions.

## Procedure

1. Name the decision, executor, independence requirement, and completion contract.
2. Select only applicable authority, constraints, accepted decisions, representative precedent, current evidence, and unresolved questions.
3. Preserve provenance and label facts, inferences, proposals, and unknowns.
4. For independent review, omit the author's persuasive narrative and provide source-derived evidence plus the review question.
5. Summarize large artifacts with pointers and digests; do not duplicate full logs or unrelated context.
6. Identify what may be discarded after durable results are persisted.

## Completion contract

Every included item is relevant and attributable, missing evidence is explicit, the packet fits its declared budget, and review independence is preserved when required.
