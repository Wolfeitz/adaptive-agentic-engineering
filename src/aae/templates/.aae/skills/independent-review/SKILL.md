# Independent Review

## Outcome

Produce an independent, evidence-cited assessment of whether the artifact satisfies its authority, architecture, safety, and completion contracts.

## Procedure

1. Use a fresh bounded packet containing source-derived authority, the artifact or diff, and verification evidence; exclude persuasive author narrative.
2. Reconstruct the intended outcome and material trust, data, lifecycle, compatibility, and operational boundaries.
3. Inspect changed behavior and relevant call paths rather than reviewing the diff in isolation.
4. Seek counterexamples, missing failure paths, false completion claims, and evidence that does not exercise the real boundary.
5. Classify findings by consequence and cite exact evidence. Separate defects from questions and optional improvements.
6. Issue a verdict without rewriting the implementation unless separately authorized.

## Completion contract

Findings are reproducible and source-cited, independence is documented, material boundaries and completion claims were challenged, and the verdict identifies blocking defects and residual uncertainty.
