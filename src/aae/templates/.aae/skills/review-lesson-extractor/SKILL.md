# Review Lesson Extractor

## Outcome

Propose durable guidance, skill candidates, or deterministic controls justified by completed outcome evidence, without self-authorizing any change to project authority.

## Procedure

1. Use completed-task and review evidence, not speculative intermediate impressions.
2. Separate project-specific facts, reusable engineering lessons, repeated procedures, and enforceable deterministic rules.
3. For a skill candidate, identify repeated instances, common inputs and outputs, a stable procedure, completion contract, expected benefit, and likely overlap.
4. Prefer deterministic enforcement when agent judgment is unnecessary.
5. Define scope, owner, validation evidence, expiry or review condition, and conflicts with current guidance.
6. Submit proposals to the configured human or governance process; do not modify authoritative guidance implicitly.

## Completion contract

Each proposal cites outcome evidence, has explicit scope and ownership, distinguishes skill from guidance and enforcement, records uncertainty and overlap, and remains unpromoted pending approval.
